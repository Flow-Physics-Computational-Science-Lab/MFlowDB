EXAFLOW3D

Conservative diffuse interfaces

ExaFlow3D is an in-house GPU-accelerated finite-volume solver. Its incompressible formulation uses staggered Cartesian grids, second-order skew-symmetric convection, fourth-order Runge–Kutta time integration, and the accurate conservative diffuse-interface (ACDI) method.

References

  1. Suhas S. Jain (2022). Accurate conservative phase-field method for simulation of two-phase flows. Journal of Computational Physics, 469, 111529.
  2. Suhas S. Jain and Ahmed Elnahhas (2025). Stationary states of forced two-phase turbulence. Chemical Engineering Journal, 524, 169077.

EXPERIMENTS

Bubble imaging and velocity measurements

The experimental collection characterizes turbulent bubbly flow in a vertical duct using shadowgraph imaging and planar, two-component liquid velocity measurements.

Shadowgraph images provide bubble measurements and size distributions. Velocity fields support derived turbulence statistics. Raw and processed records are organized by operating condition and spatial location.

Reference

  1. Vivek Kumar, Prasoon Suchandra, Ardalan Javadi, Suhas S. Jain, and Cyrus K. Aidun (2026). Bubble coalescence dynamics in a high-Reynolds number decaying turbulent flow. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 1033, A6.
