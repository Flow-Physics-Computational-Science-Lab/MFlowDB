BUILT ON RESOLVED PHYSICS

Two methods. A shared purpose.

Resolve turbulent motion and deformable interfaces to support model development and systematic comparison.

EXAFLOW3D

Conservative diffuse interfaces

ExaFlow3D is an in-house GPU-accelerated finite-volume solver. Its incompressible formulation uses staggered Cartesian grids, second-order skew-symmetric convection, fourth-order Runge–Kutta time integration, and the accurate conservative diffuse-interface (ACDI) method.

NEKRS-LS

High-order level-set methods

A hybrid conservative level-set approach uses a GPU-accelerated continuous Galerkin spectral element method with spectrally vanishing viscosity stabilization.
